Elections, Reimagined.

Every college has elections and campaigning and what not! But what if there was a platform where you could do everything? Welcome again to Votexa!

Votexa is an AI-powered election management platform designed to make organizational elections simpler, smarter and more transparent.

What can you do with Votexa?

  • Create elections
  • Register candidates
  • Manage voters
  • Cast votes securely
  • View election results
